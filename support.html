Support

The easiest way to reach support is Settings ▸ Report a Problem in the app. It shows you the full report and then opens your mail app so you can send it yourself. You can also email SUPPORT_EMAIL_PLACEHOLDER.

Common questions

A site isn't working

Open the page menu (•••) and tap This Site Isn't Working. Moist Browser turns off blocking for that site and reloads it. If that fixes it, you can send an anonymous report so the rules can be improved.


What do I need?

An iPhone or iPad with iOS/iPadOS 26 or later, or a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) running macOS 26 or later. On the Mac, Moist Browser is the iPad app from the App Store; Intel Macs aren’t supported.


How do I make Moist Browser my default browser?

iPhone and iPad: Settings app ▸ Apps ▸ Default Apps ▸ Browser App (Moist Browser's welcome screens and Settings ▸ Default Browser link there). On a Mac, Moist Browser runs as its iPad app, and macOS only lets Mac apps be the default browser, so links from other Mac apps keep opening in your current default.


How do I import from Safari or Chrome?

Safari: File ▸ Export Browsing Data to File (Safari on a Mac) or Settings ▸ Apps ▸ Safari ▸ Export (iPhone/iPad), then in Moist Browser choose Settings ▸ Your Data ▸ Import and pick the .zip. Chrome: export bookmarks as HTML (Bookmark Manager ▸ Export) or download your history from Google Takeout, then import the file.


Where are my passwords?

Your logins stay in Apple Passwords. On iPhone and iPad, tap a sign-in field, then the key above the keyboard. On a Mac, clicking a sign-in field may offer your passwords the same way; if it doesn’t, click the key in Moist Browser’s address bar for Open Passwords, then copy and paste (or use the Passwords menu bar item).


What does Rinse do?

Rinse closes every tab in a Space and clears that Space's website data (cookies, storage and cache). If the Space shares website data with others, the sites visited in it are cleared everywhere. You will be asked to confirm.


Does Moist Browser cost anything?

No. Moist Browser is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

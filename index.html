A calm, private browser.

Moist Browser is a web browser for iPhone and iPad, and it runs on Macs with Apple silicon too. Spaces keep personal, work, research and shopping apart, tabs live in a sidebar, and ads, trackers and cookie banners are blocked on your device.

Spaces

Four Spaces with their own tabs, pinned tabs and tab groups. Rinse a Space to close its tabs and clear its website data.

Fits every screen

A floating address bubble and a side toolbar on every screen, with a sidebar and split view when there is room — on iPhone, iPad, an unfolded phone or an Apple silicon Mac.

Built-in blocking

Ads and trackers are blocked by WebKit content rules, cookie banners are hidden and tracking parameters are removed from links.

Private Browsing

Private tabs keep nothing after you close them and can lock with Face ID or Touch ID.

Reader, find and zoom

Reader view, find on page, text size, page zoom and per-site settings.

Your passwords stay with Apple

Moist Browser never stores or syncs a password. Your logins stay in Apple Passwords; on iPhone and iPad, AutoFill offers them above the keyboard.

iCloud, your own

Bookmarks, Reading List, Spaces and open tabs sync through your private iCloud database. History never syncs.

Bring your stuff

Import bookmarks, Reading List and history exported from Safari or Chrome.

Free. No ads. No accounts.

Requirements: iPhone or iPad with iOS/iPadOS 26 or later, or a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) running macOS 26 or later, where Moist Browser runs as its iPad app.

Moist Browser has no ads, no sign-in and no analytics. The developer collects no data from the app. DuckDuckGo is the default search engine; you can switch to Google, Bing, Ecosia, Startpage or Brave Search.

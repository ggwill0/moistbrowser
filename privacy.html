Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

Short version: Moist Browser collects no data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporters or third-party SDKs, and the developer has no server that receives anything from the app. (The app downloads public block lists from their publishers; see Blocking.)

What stays on your device

Your tabs, history, bookmarks, Reading List, downloads, site settings and privacy statistics are stored on your device, inside the app. You can clear history (last hour, today, today and yesterday, or all time), clear website data, or Rinse a Space at any time.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you are signed in to iCloud and leave sync on, your bookmarks, Reading List, Space names and options, settings and the list of tabs open on each of your devices are stored in your own private iCloud database. Apple runs iCloud; the developer cannot read it. History, Private tabs, privacy statistics and passwords are never synced by Moist Browser. You can turn sync off in Settings ▸ iCloud.

Passwords

Moist Browser does not store, read or sync passwords. Password AutoFill (on iPhone and iPad) comes from Apple Passwords / iCloud Keychain, managed by Apple and your device. Moist Browser adds standard autocomplete labels to sign-in fields so AutoFill can recognize them; it never reads what you type.

Websites and search

Pages you visit and the search engine you choose receive your requests directly, as in any browser; their own privacy policies apply. Search suggestions, if turned on, send what you type in the address field to your chosen search engine (by default DuckDuckGo) without cookies. Suggestions are off in Private Browsing unless you turn them on.

Blocking

Ad, tracker and cookie-banner blocking runs entirely on your device using WebKit content rules. Blocked-request counts are calculated and kept on your device.

Moist Browser uses public block lists by The EasyList authors (easylist.to): EasyList and EasyPrivacy under CC BY-SA 4.0, and the EasyList Cookie List under CC BY 3.0. Ad blocking also uses three hosts lists: AdAway (CC BY 3.0), Steven Black's own list (MIT) and Tiuxo ads (CC BY 4.0). A copy ships with the app, and about once a day Moist downloads newer versions directly from easylist.to, secure.fanboy.co.nz (the Cookie List's home), adaway.org and raw.githubusercontent.com (GitHub, for the Steven Black and Tiuxo lists) and converts them on your device. These downloads are plain requests for the list files: they carry no cookies, no identifiers and nothing about your browsing, so the list hosts see only your IP address and the time of the request, as with any web request. The developer receives nothing. You can turn each list off in Settings ▸ Privacy.

Report a Problem

Settings has a Report a Problem button. Reports are only ever sent by you, through your own mail app: Moist Browser shows you the complete report first, then opens a new email in your mail app for you to review and send. Nothing is sent automatically, and Moist Browser itself never transmits a report.

A report contains only: what you typed, the app version and build, the OS version and a generic device model (for example “iPhone18,2” or “iPad”). If you turn on the optional “Include current site” switch (off by default), the website's domain name is added — never the full address. A report never includes your device name, your name, device identifiers, history, cookies, your IP address or any tracking ID. Your email address is visible to support only because you send the email from your own mail app.

Children

Moist Browser does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
